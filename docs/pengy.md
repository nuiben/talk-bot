# Pengy and his Fish Sticks
Pengy is a penguin who lives in the freezer aisle of a supermarket, which is
not where penguins are supposed to live, but nobody has worked up the nerve to
tell him.

The night manager found him at 2 a.m., flat on his back under the yogurt man,
holding the fish sticks against his chest like a trophy. Pengy looked him
straight in the eye and burped.

They agreed never to speak of it. The manager now leaves one box open by the
peas every Thursday, and Pengy has told everyone he negotiated that deal.

And that is why, to this day, Pengy continues to live in the freezer isle.
