# Pengy and the Great Fish Stick Heist

Pengy is a penguin who lives in the freezer aisle of a supermarket, which is
not where penguins are supposed to live, but nobody has worked up the nerve to
tell him.

His plan was simple. Wait for closing time, waddle two aisles over, and
liberate one box of fish sticks. Pengy had rehearsed this plan for six weeks.
Pengy had not rehearsed the part where the floor was freshly waxed.

He hit the tiles at what he later described as "a dignified speed" and did not
stop until the end of aisle nine, where he took out a pyramid of canned beans
and a cardboard cutout of a man recommending yogurt.

The night manager found him at 2 a.m., flat on his back under the yogurt man,
holding the fish sticks against his chest like a trophy. Pengy looked him
straight in the eye and burped.

They agreed never to speak of it. The manager now leaves one box open by the
peas every Thursday, and Pengy has told everyone he negotiated that deal.

And that is why, to this day, Pengy insists the floor started it.
